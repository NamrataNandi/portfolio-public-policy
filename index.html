About

I work at the intersection of governance and research. Most recently, I served as a Labour Inspector (Gazetted) under the Government of Assam, where I handled regulatory compliance, welfare-scheme data systems, and multi-agency coordination for programmes reaching thousands of workers across the district, where I was posted in.

I hold a B.A. in Economics — where I graduated as Chancellor's Gold Medalist — and a postgraduate degree in Data Science from IIIT Bangalore. I'm now applying that combination of quantitative training and on-the-ground governance experience to public policy and evidence-based research.
